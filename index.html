Evac sector 7

Dead Mile

Get across the city to the helipad on the far side and hold it until the chopper lands. The dead keep coming, and the longer you take, the faster they come.

  • WASD or Arrows to move. Shift to sprint. Q to drop a flare.
  • E at a doorway to shut or open it. E at a car to set off its alarm.
  • Drag anywhere to move. Hold SPRINT to run. The button above it changes to SHUT, SEARCH or ALARM. Tap FLARE to drop one.
  • A car alarm pulls the dead outside toward it for 8 seconds. One use per car.
  • Search cabinets, fridges, dressers and shelves for flares, planks, medkits, batteries and adrenaline. A flare pulls the dead toward its light. Planks board up any door you shut.
  • Every building can be entered. Shut doors buy time, but the dead will break them.

Know the dead

  • WalkerSlow and endless. Most of them.
  • CrawlerLow to the ground. Eyes barely show.
  • RunnerFaster than you walk. Sprint.
  • ScreamerSpots you, then calls every dead thing nearby.
  • BloaterBursts up close. Kills other dead too.
  • BruteHits for two hearts. Smashes doors.

And one more. After 40 seconds, something pale starts following you. It only moves when no light is on it: your flashlight, a flare or a working lamp. If the edges of the screen start to pulse, turn around.
